# 2027 rookie class — dynasty, 1QB PPR

_Researched 2026-10-01 (about week 4-5 of the college season) by a research agent. 2026 YTD stats through
~9/30/26, mostly from Jordan Reid's ESPN board (10/1/26). Most dates/stats came via a summarizing page reader —
spot-check before a trade depends on one. Not checked: KTC, FantasyPros, Underdog, The Athletic/Brugler,
NFL.com/Jeremiah. * = underclassman who can return to school._

**Bottom line:** WR- and RB-driven for 1QB. Jeremiah Smith is the consensus 1.01. WR is the deepest group, RB
is strong (a bounce-back from the weak 2026 class), QBs are strong for the NFL but go in round 2 of 1QB rookie
drafts, and TE is a two-man class.

## Class strength
- **WR: strong, the best group.** Reid has 8 WRs in his top 50; SI lists WR among the 4 strongest positions; DraftSharks "transformative". Smith called "generational" (SI), compared to Julio Jones.
- **QB: strong for the NFL, minor in 1QB.** Order is contested (Reid: Mestemaker; Dynasty Nerds/DraftSharks: Moore; CBS preseason: Manning; PFF preseason: Sayin). Manning and Moore are cooling.
- **RB: above average.** Fantasy Footballers (preseason) "strong, bounce-back"; Dynasty Nerds "DEEP". NFL boards are cooler (only Baugh in Reid's top 50).
- **TE: average to weak.** Johnson and Green, then a drop to round 3; both missed time.

## 1QB rookie draft shape
1. **Jeremiah Smith, 1.01** (Dynasty Nerds 9/15 mock + 9/22 board, DraftSharks 10/1). No dissent.
2. **1.02-1.03 open: Cam Coleman, Jadan Baugh, Kewan Lacy.** Baugh's start and Lacy's shoulder favour Baugh now; Becker a dark horse.
3. **QBs fall to round 2** (Dynasty Nerds mock: Manning 2.05, Moore 2.08).

## Uncertainties
Return-to-school (Manning, Mensah, Mestemaker, Sayin, Carr, Baugh, Lacy, Hardy, Becker, Marsh, Green); injuries
(Moore, Lacy, Green, Johnson, Hardy); 4-game samples (Duff, Mensah, Baugh). Smith's yards differ by source (617 vs 607).
