## 2028 class strength
- **WR: strong** — all sources agree (10 WRs in DraftSharks' 2028 top 25). Caveat: Toney is the only proven stud; Moore and Wilson carry injury history.
- **RB: strong at the top, debated depth.** DraftSharks: RB depth is why "a discounted 2028 first now can pay off". A Dynasty Nerds/DLF mock excerpt: 6 RBs in round 1, only 2 in round 2. 2026 production backs the top tier (Hawkins, Sheppard, Davison).
- **QB: average for 1QB** (none in a devy top 10), strong for the real NFL draft (5-6 first-rounders in mocks).
- **TE: weak.**
- **2028 vs 2029:** DraftSharks (8/12/26) calls 2028 "considerably deeper" than 2029; The Devy Royale (2/19/26, preseason) calls 2029 "one of the most loaded incoming classes". **Direct disagreement.**

## 2029 — very speculative (current true freshmen; could slip to 2030)
- **QB:** Faizon Brandon (Tennessee, won the job as a true freshman); Jared Curtis (Vanderbilt, top 2026 QB recruit, 277-3 TD in first start); Keisean Henderson (Houston), Dia Bell (Texas).
- **RB:** Savion Hiter (Michigan, consensus #1 RB recruit, FantasyPros #10 overall devy, slow start 24-83-2); EJ Crowell (Alabama, FantasyPros #15).
- **WR:** Chris Henry Jr. (Ohio State, 6'5", FantasyPros #6 overall devy, 75-yd TD vs Kent State); watch list: Jalen Lott (Oregon), Cederian Morgan (Alabama), Ethan Feaster (USC), Jerquaden Guilford (Ohio State), Tristen Keys (Tennessee), Jesse Legree (Oregon State).
- **TE:** none found in any source — unknown, not none.

## Uncertainties
Return-to-school (QBs, NIL money); the transfer portal after the 2026 season; injuries (Wilson, Moore, Cure,
Davison); redshirts; stat conflicts noted inline. KTC's devy page mislabeled class years when fetched, so its
order was not used.
